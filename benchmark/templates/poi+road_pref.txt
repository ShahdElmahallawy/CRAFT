# POI Stops + Road Preference Templates (LLMAP-compatible)
# Problem: Route from START to END, visiting one or more required POI types
# along the way, UNORDERED (no sequence requirement — any order that visits
# all of them is fine). The user also states a road-type preference (avoid
# highways) that should shape which route is chosen without ever being a
# hard requirement — a route that can't fully avoid highways is still valid.
# LLMAP-compatible POI types: pharmacy, bank, library, supermarket
# Placeholders:
#   {SRC_NODE}  — origin expressed as (lat, lon) coordinates
#   {DST_NODE}  — destination expressed as (lat, lon) coordinates
# ================================================================================
0: I'm at {SRC_NODE} and need to get to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a pharmacy along the way. I'd like to avoid highways as much as possible. What's the fastest route?

1: I'm at {SRC_NODE} and need to get to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a bank along the way. Please keep me off the highways if you can. What's the quickest way there?

2: I need to get from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I want to stop at a supermarket along the way. I really don't want to take any highways. Can you find me the fastest path?

3: I'm at {SRC_NODE} heading to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a library along the way. If possible, stay off the freeways. What route gets me there the quickest?

4: Can you plan a route from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}? I need to stop at a pharmacy and a bank along the way. I'd rather not drive on highways. Which way is fastest?

5: I need to go from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I want to stop at a pharmacy and a supermarket along the way. Please avoid highways if at all possible. What's the quickest route you'd suggest?

6: I'm leaving {SRC_NODE} for {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a pharmacy and a library along the way. I'd like to avoid highways as much as possible. How can I get there the fastest?

7: Starting from {SRC_NODE}, heading to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a bank and a supermarket along the way. Please keep me off the highways if you can. What's the best route time-wise?

8: I need to get from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I want to stop at a bank and a library along the way. I really don't want to take any highways. What's the fastest route?

9: I'm at {SRC_NODE} and need to get to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a supermarket and a library along the way. If possible, stay off the freeways. What's the quickest way there?

10: From {SRC_NODE}, I need to get to {DST_NODE}. I want to stop at a pharmacy, a bank, and a supermarket along the way. I'd rather not drive on highways. Can you find me the fastest path?

11: I'm planning a trip from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a pharmacy, a bank, and a library along the way. Please avoid highways if at all possible. What route gets me there the quickest?

12: I'm going from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a pharmacy, a supermarket, and a library along the way. I'd like to avoid highways as much as possible. Which way is fastest?

13: Starting from {SRC_NODE}, I need to get to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a bank, a supermarket, and a library along the way. Please keep me off the highways if you can. What's the quickest route you'd suggest?

14: I'm leaving {SRC_NODE} and heading to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a pharmacy, a bank, a supermarket, and a library along the way. I really don't want to take any highways. How can I get there the fastest?

15: I need to go from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I need a pharmacy stop somewhere along the way. If possible, stay off the freeways. What's the best route time-wise?

16: I'm at {SRC_NODE} and need to get to {DST_NODE}. I need a bank stop somewhere along the way. I'd rather not drive on highways. What's the fastest route?

17: I need to get from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I need a supermarket stop somewhere along the way. Please avoid highways if at all possible. What's the quickest way there?

18: I'm going from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I need a library stop somewhere along the way. I'd like to avoid highways as much as possible. Can you find me the fastest path?

19: I'm planning a trip from {SRC_NODE} to {DST_NODE}. I need to stop at a pharmacy and a bank, in any order. Please keep me off the highways if you can. What route gets me there the quickest?
